# Stream Previews v0.6.1 - AMO Release Notes

What's New in Version 0.6.1:

- Refined Range Slider Styling:
  Updated range slider thumbs to a modern, compact dark-core knob with crisp white ring delineation and subtle elevation shadow.

- Brighter Slider Track Visibility:
  Enhanced range slider tracks with higher-contrast slate backgrounds and subtle bordering for improved visibility across dark themes.

- Ultra-Smooth Slider Adjustments:
  Refined slider step granularities (1% increments on volume, 5px increments on preview width, and 10ms increments on hover trigger delay) for completely smooth, fluid dragging without jerky movement.
